# Recovery trigger learning

- Preserve the exact accepted-send receipt, task/hop ownership, and conversation URL while releasing a blocked task; recovery must resume durable work rather than replay it. Treat any non-empty composer on an unbound or mismatched tab as operator-owned by default; classify it as stale automation residue only when its full normalized text exactly matches an immutable durable request already accepted or completed elsewhere, and cleanup still requires authority over the affected task.
- Treat immediate release, durable source repair, concrete defect recording, and reusable learning as separate decisions; `PROBLEM.md` holds incident evidence while trigger learning holds only generalized invariants, and report recovery must preserve canonical role-turn evidence, never overwrite different existing content, and consume a proven accepted response without replay.
- Claim only continuously blocked work after its eligibility window, process the oldest eligible target one at a time, and apply per-target plus inter-target cooldowns to prevent recovery thrash.
- Classify transport failure from bounded UI/error evidence rather than arbitrary phrases quoted inside role content, and distinguish pre-durable send preparation from a crossed accepted-send boundary before blocking or replaying recovery. The exact RequestLedger, not the hop label alone, is authoritative for that boundary: a hop labeled `sending` whose exact ledger is still `NEW` with zero attempts, no binding, and no receipt remains pre-durable and may safely reacquire ownership before the first Send. Conversely, a durable `SENDING` record without an accepted receipt is an ambiguous boundary: loss of a provisional or ephemeral source page is neither proof of failure nor permission to restart. First prove accepted-user provenance or provable non-acceptance; if neither is available, preserve the exact hop/request and hold it for repair instead of replaying it.
- Do not treat a role-report file existing or having an early mtime as proof that the assistant turn is complete; tool-using roles may write the report while response activity continues. Diagnose completion from the exact durable response/stable final assistant turn plus inactive transport, and treat recent semantic response-activity changes as ongoing work. Progress fingerprints must be canonical across lightweight and full probe representations: representation-only differences must not extend no-progress budgets. Once that stable accepted response is proven complete—or a completed backend turn remains semantically unchanged through one bounded recovery—route response and validation failures through the same bounded repair state machine rather than leaving the durable hop stranded or replaying it.
- When an accepted waiting hop has exact durable backend identity, recovery is backend-first even when the original response deadline has expired: rearm one bounded stream-status/graph recovery window before requiring the source tab. Treat exact-tab reopening as DOM fallback only, and never automatically Restart or resend an already accepted request. Before classifying an accepted-hop page-ownership mismatch, reconcile a provisional `WEB:*` identity from an immutable-matching durable RequestLedger receipt when it has since gained the canonical conversation ID; this is identity enrichment of the same accepted send, never permission to rebind another page or replay the request. Bootstrap donors are read-only anchors: a writable role branch is not safe to Send until its eventual canonical conversation is proven distinct from the donor and from other writable task/role ownership. If a proven accepted turn later becomes historical only because another durable workflow reused that conversation, recover the exact prior terminal segment only with cross-request durable provenance; arbitrary later-human steering remains fail-closed.
